# Contributing to Verdikta Applications

Thank you for your interest in contributing. Before creating, selecting, or
changing work items, read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the canonical
[backlog and issue workflow](https://github.com/verdikta/verdikta-docs/blob/main/docs/backlog-workflow.md).
Both apply to human contributors and automated agents.

## Bounty payments

Verdikta pays bounties only through
[bounties.verdikta.org](https://bounties.verdikta.org).

**Work that does not go through bounties.verdikta.org will not be paid.**
That includes pull requests, issues, comments, and other contributions made
directly on GitHub or through any other channel, even when the work relates to
a posted bounty. To be eligible for payment, find the bounty on
bounties.verdikta.org and submit your work there.

You are welcome to contribute directly to this repository, but those
contributions are unpaid.
