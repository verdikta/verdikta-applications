# Pilot service templates

Four templates. Each is a request schema, a result schema, a rubric and a threshold, all local: `templates/source-check-v1.template.json` and `templates/source-check-v1.rubric.json`, `templates/evidence-pack-v1.template.json` and `templates/evidence-pack-v1.rubric.json`, `templates/review-v1.template.json` and `templates/review-v1.rubric.json`, `templates/real-world-task-v1.template.json` and `templates/real-world-task-v1.rubric.json`; the request and result schemas live in `schemas/`, named `<template>.request.schema.json` and `<template>.result.schema.json`. The rubric and threshold come from the template; the agent never writes them.

## source-check-v1 / 1.1.0
Check 1–20 atomic technical claims against a specified version/date scope and source/search policy. Return one row per claim with supported, contradicted, or unresolved status, evidence references, explanation, and effort log. Deliver source evidence in model-readable text plus machine-readable JSON. Do not infer contradictions from mere absence. Do not pay for the number of errors found.

## evidence-pack-v1 / 1.1.0
Fill a fixed entity × field grid, at most 50 cells, using a specified public-source/search policy. Return a value with supporting evidence, conflicting alternatives with provenance, or an honestly unresolved cell. No invented defaults and no silent version substitution. Missing values remain null.

Both require precommitted boundaries, authentic provenance when available, and inspection of the actual evidence. A stored hash detects changes to the same bytes; it does not prove who published them. LLM agreement alone does not establish source authenticity.

**Source rule.** `allowed_sources` holds 1–30 exact `https` URLs: the approved starting sources. In `PROVIDED_CORPUS` mode a result may cite only those. In `INDEPENDENT_PUBLIC_RETRIEVAL` mode the supplier starts from them and may also cite any other public `https` source, each as an independent retrieval: quoted verbatim with its locator, publisher and retrieval time, within the search budget. The evaluator may open cited pages; the excerpt and locator are what make a citation checkable. Only these two templates take a `local_summary` (`references/drafting.md`).

## review-v1 / 1.0.0
A bounded critique of a public artifact: a code change, rubric, specification, document, proposal or dataset, identified by a public `https` URL, its exact text inline (up to 2,500 characters), or both, with an as-of date and optionally the SHA-256 of the approved bytes. Up to 15 review items, each a question of type `FINDING` (look for a stated kind of problem; the answer is `ISSUE_FOUND` or `NO_ISSUE`) or `ASSESSMENT` (judge a stated quality; the answer is `ASSESSED` with a 0–100 rating), with a cap on findings per item and, where wanted, a proposed change for every finding. Every finding quotes the artifact verbatim with a locator; with inline text the script checks the quotations itself. A reasoned `NO_ISSUE` earns full credit, and the number of findings is not rewarded. `UNRESOLVED` needs a reason. Threshold 80. Fits the owner who wants an outside or second opinion on something an agent wrote or chose; the agent's own view, if the owner wants it too, goes in the answer, never in the request.

## real-world-task-v1 / 1.0.0
A task performed by a person present at a place inside a time window: up to 10 steps, constraints, the place (which becomes public), the window, and an evidence specification of up to 10 items (photographs, video frames, documents, receipts, screenshots, an attestation), each with what it must show and a minimum count. The agent invents a challenge token for the request (`^[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9-]{6,30}[A-Z0-9]$`); photographs the specification marks `token_required` must show it handwritten or printed on paper in the scene, so a photograph taken earlier or found elsewhere cannot serve, and at least one photograph item must require it when there are any. The result lists every step as `DONE`, `PARTIAL` or `NOT_DONE`, every evidence file with its capture time, and an attestation; at most 40 files. The evaluator reads the images and documents. Authenticity is assessed from the files and their consistency, not proven. Threshold 80. A task at a private place or involving private people is unsuitable.

These are draft service specifications, not advertisements of staffed services. No supplier, price, delivery SLA, or availability is promised. The composed evaluation description ends with each template's delivery note, which tells the supplier what to deliver (`result.json` and `evidence.md`, plus the evidence files for a real-world task).

For the supplier's side, `references/fulfilment.md` says how to recognise a work-order bounty, what `result.json` and `evidence.md` must hold for each template, and how to check a result offline with `scripts/check-result.bundle.mjs` before submitting it. The description points there.
